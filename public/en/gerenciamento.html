      Understand the calculations

      The cash stop is the planned maximum loss for the day's single trade. The days show how many full stops the budget covers, capped at 20. Targets are 2 and 3 times the cash stop. The table only shows contract possibilities for each stop distance; it does not select a trade.

      The quote is used for notional value and exposure; sizing contracts by stop points does not depend on it. Exposure / budget is not your broker's margin requirement. Estimates exclude costs, trading taxes and execution differences.
